# Créditos

- **The Knight**: sprites extraídos de **Hollow Knight** (frames em
  `assets/sprites/character/the_knight/`).
- **Hornet**: sprites extraídos de **Hollow Knight / Hollow Knight: Silksong**
  (`assets/sprites/character/Hornet/`), incluindo os atlas "Hornet main sprites" (movimentos) e
  "Hornet needolin" (tocando a agulha).
- **Sombra (Shade)**, **banco** (`items/town_bench_lit.png`) e **poste** (`items/cliffs_lamp.png`):
  arte de **Hollow Knight**.
- **Grub**: folha "Hollow Knight — Non-Playable Characters — Grub" (The Spriters Resource),
  em `assets/sprites/character/Grup/`.
